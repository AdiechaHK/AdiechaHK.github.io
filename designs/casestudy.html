Moving 36GB of production data with the lights on

WebOccult · 2025
Problem

A production MongoDB at 250–300 concurrent connections — web services, batch jobs, edge devices — had begun starving the services sharing its host.

Approach

Replica-set promotion with a scripted cutover: quiesce writers, verify sync, promote the secondary, rotate credentials, restart. Scripted because a cutover you perform by hand is a cutover you perform differently under pressure.

→

Ten seconds of total downtime, plus one planned twenty-minute window.

When the client broke the contract our data model rested on

Improwised · 2019–2022
Problem

An eight-year-old scraping platform assumed client-supplied IDs were stable. A client began emitting inconsistent identifiers for the same business, and the assumption collapsed underneath us.

Approach

Rather than rebuild, I added an MD5 content hash over the business's identifying attributes as a fallback key, checked only when the primary ID misses — restoring integrity without breaking a single existing client API contract.

→

Eliminated 95% of duplicate business records.
