36GB MongoDB, moved live

WebOccult · production migration · 2025

Completed
one planned window10 sec unplanned · 20 min planned

A production MongoDB at 250–300 concurrent connections — web services, batch jobs, edge devices — had begun starving the services sharing its host. I moved it onto dedicated infrastructure by replica-set promotion with a scripted cutover: quiesce writers, verify sync, promote the secondary, rotate credentials, restart. Scripted, because a cutover you perform by hand is a cutover you perform differently under pressure.

Result

Ten seconds of total downtime, plus one planned twenty-minute window.

Deduplication, after the contract broke

Improwised · 8-year-old platform · 2019–2022

Recovered
integrity restored95% of duplicates eliminated

The platform assumed client-supplied IDs were stable. A client began emitting inconsistent identifiers for the same business and the assumption collapsed underneath us. Rather than rebuild, I added an MD5 content hash over the business's identifying attributes as a fallback key, checked only when the primary ID misses — restoring integrity without breaking a single existing client API contract.

Result

Eliminated 95% of duplicate business records.
