Ahmedabad

2025

In production

An agent that writes the reports nobody wanted to write

Situation

Developer status reports and monthly client progress reports were manual, slow, and easy to get subtly wrong.

What I did

Built a Claude Code workflow pulling from Odoo and GitHub, with per-item evidence attached to every claim. The real design question was never "can the model write this" — it was which judgments stay with a human. The agent gathers and drafts; people decide.

Outcome

Status reports 30–45 min → ~5 min. Monthly client reports: a full working day → under 30 minutes, including revisions.

Ahmedabad

Live migration
2025

10s downtime

Moving 36GB of production data with the lights on

Situation

A production MongoDB at 250–300 concurrent connections — web services, batch jobs, edge devices — had begun starving the services sharing its host.

What I did

Moved it onto dedicated infrastructure by replica-set promotion with a scripted cutover: quiesce writers, verify sync, promote the secondary, rotate credentials, restart. Scripted, because a cutover you perform by hand is a cutover you perform differently under pressure.

Outcome

Ten seconds of total downtime, plus one planned twenty-minute window.

Rajkot

8-year-old platform
2019–2022

95% recovered

When the client broke the contract our data model rested on

Situation

The platform assumed client-supplied IDs were stable. A client began emitting inconsistent identifiers for the same business, and the assumption collapsed underneath us.

What I did

Rather than rebuild, added an MD5 content hash over the business's identifying attributes as a fallback key, checked only when the primary ID misses — restoring integrity without breaking a single existing client API contract.

Outcome

Eliminated 95% of duplicate business records.

500K requests a day, and a rewrite shipped into live traffic

Situation

500K requests per day across up to 300 pods against a ~200GB database on rolling three-month retention — and the core scraper was the bottleneck.

What I did

Migrated it from Python to Go and rolled the rewrite into the running large-scale system rather than staging a parallel cutover.
